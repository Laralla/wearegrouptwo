# 🌍 Climate Change: The World's Greatest Cost

An interactive website exploring climate change as a problem of global
interdependence, made for our **Contemporary World** class (Group 2).

## 🔗 Live site
https://laralla.github.io/wearegrouptwo/

> Copy the exact address from **Settings → Pages** ("Your site is live at…")
> and paste it here in place of the link above.

## 🧭 What's inside
- **Introduction:** what climate change is, why it's happening, and its effects
- **Roll the Earth:** a draggable, zoomable 3D globe with ASEAN and world regions
- **Spotlight: ASEAN:** the climate risks of each member state, with source links
- **Cause and Effect:** how emissions lead to extreme weather, food shortages,
  migration and economic damage
- **Who Is Involved:** governments, corporations, financial institutions, civil
  society and citizens, plus a chain-reaction demo
- **Virtual Courtroom:** weigh the evidence and deliver a verdict on who is
  responsible
- **Ways Forward:** actions the world can take together

## 💡 Central idea
In a world where countries are interconnected, global problems require us to
look beyond individual interests and consider our shared responsibilities.
