# 🌍 Climate Change: The World's Greatest Cost

An interactive website that explores climate change as a problem of global
interdependence, with a special focus on ASEAN. It was made for our
**Contemporary World** class (Group 2).

🔗 **Live site:** https://laralla.github.io/wearegrouptwo/

---

## 💡 Central idea
In a world where countries are interconnected, global problems require us to
look beyond individual interests and consider our shared responsibilities.

## 🧭 What's inside
| Section | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Introduction** | What climate change is, why it's happening, and its effects |
| **Roll the Earth** | A draggable, zoomable 3D globe showing ASEAN and other world regions |
| **Spotlight: ASEAN** | Flag cards with the climate risks of each member state and source links |
| **Cause and Effect** | How emissions lead to extreme weather, food shortages, migration and economic damage |
| **Who Is Involved** | Governments, corporations, financial institutions, civil society and citizens, with a chain-reaction demo |
| **Virtual Courtroom** | Weigh the evidence, read the counterarguments and deliver a verdict on who is responsible |
| **Ways Forward** | Actions the world can take together |
